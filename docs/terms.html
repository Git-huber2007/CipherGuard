Operational Guidelines

Terms of Engagement & Operational Security

Effective Date: September 2026 · Rules of Engagement (RoE) · SIH-26160 Compliance

1. Scope of Authorization & Permitted Usage

CipherGuard is engineered exclusively for authorized defensive cyber operations, academic security research, and network infrastructure hardening. By launching or operating this framework, you certify that:

  • [ROE-1]
    You own, administer, or possess explicit written authorization to capture and inspect traffic on the monitored network segments or TAP interfaces.
  • [ROE-2]
    RF spectrum scans are performed strictly in passive listening mode without emitting 802.11 deauthentication frames or initiating active wireless denial-of-service attacks.

2. Passive Defense Architecture & Remediation Safeguards

The remediation engine synthesizes vendor-specific configurations (Cisco IOS-XE, StrongSwan, FortiOS) based on identified cryptographic vulnerabilities.

Mandatory Staged Approval: Generated remediation playbooks must always be reviewed by a qualified network administrator and staged in dry-run mode prior to change-window deployment. CipherGuard never applies configuration mutations to production gateways automatically without human sign-off.

3. Prohibited Conduct & Statutory Penalties

Unauthorized interception of electronic communications is a criminal offense under Section 66 and 72 of the Indian Information Technology Act (2000) and equivalent international laws. Use of CipherGuard to eavesdrop on private non-consensual communications, intercept unauthorized wireless links, or evade lawful telecommunications controls is strictly forbidden.

4. Warranty Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability

Under no circumstances shall the developers, contributors, NTRO, or partner institutions be liable for operational disruptions, misconfigurations resulting from third-party VPN firmware differences, or network downtime.
